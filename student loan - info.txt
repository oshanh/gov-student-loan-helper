# Interest-Free Student Loan Scheme (IFSLS) - Intake 11 (Academic Year 2026/2027)

---

## 1. General Minimum Admission Requirements
To be eligible to apply for any degree program under the Interest-Free Student Loan Scheme (Intake 11), candidates must satisfy all of the following conditions:

1. **A/L Examination Qualification:** Passed the G.C.E. (A/L) Examination in **2023, 2024, or 2025** conducted by the Department of Examinations, Sri Lanka, and been considered qualified for university entrance.
2. **Subject Passes:** Obtained simple (**S**) passes for all three subjects in one and the same sitting (not exceeding three sittings).
3. **Common General Test:** A minimum of **30 marks** for the Common General Test at any sitting (not exceeding three sittings).
4. **English Language:** 
   - A minimum of a simple (**S**) pass for General English at the G.C.E. (A/L) Examination; **OR**
   - A minimum of a simple (**S**) pass for English Language at the G.C.E. (O/L) Examination.
5. **Age Limit:** Must be **25 years old or below** by September 27, 2026.

### B. Management and Commerce

#### **SLIIT (Malabe, Kandy, Matara, Jaffna)**
* **Bachelor of Business Administration (Special)(Hons) (4 Years)**
  * **A/L:** Three (3) 'S' passes in Commerce stream **OR** three (3) 'S' passes in any stream with "Economics" + O/L Mathematics ('S') **OR** three (3) 'S' passes in any stream + Bridging Course (min 50%) + O/L Mathematics ('S').

#### **NSBM Green University**
* **Bachelor of Science in Business Management (Project Management) (Special) (4 Years)**
  * **A/L:** Three (3) 'S' passes in Commerce stream (or any stream with Economics); 'S' pass in General English.
  * **O/L:** 'S' pass in Mathematics and 'C' pass in English (Bridging courses available for non-commerce streams).

#### **ICASL (Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka)**
* **BSc (Hons) / BSc in Applied Accounting / BMgmt (Hons) / BMgmt in Business Analytics (3-4 Years)**
  * **A/L:** Three (3) 'S' passes in Commerce stream (with Accounting) **OR** three (3) 'S' passes in any stream + O/L 'C' passes in Mathematics and Business & Accounting Studies.

#### **CINEC Campus**
* **BBM (Hons) in Marketing / Supply Chain / Business Admin / HRM / Banking & Finance / Accounting (4 Years)**
  * **A/L:** Three (3) 'S' passes in Commerce stream (or stream with Economics/Accounting) subject to O/L Mathematics/Accounting requirements or Bridging Courses.

#### **SIBA Campus / Horizon Campus / KIU / SLTC / Saegis Campus / BCI Campus / ICBT / ESOFT / BMS**
* **Bachelor of Business Management (Honours) / Specializations (3-4 Years)**
  * **A/L:** Three (3) 'S' passes in Commerce stream (or arts/science streams containing Economics or Accounting).
  * **Bridging:** Candidates from general streams without Economics/Accounting usually require a credit in O/L Mathematics/Accounting or completion of an approved university bridging course.

### E. Information and Communication Technology (Computing Stream)

*Applies to SLIIT, NSBM, CINEC, Horizon, KIU, Saegis, SLTC, ESOFT, BCI, ICBT, NIIBS, and SIBA.*

* **Degree Titles:** 
  * BSc (Hons) in Software Engineering
  * BSc in Information Technology / Computer Science
  * BSc (Hons) in Cyber Security / Cloud Computing / Data Science / Network Security
  * BSc in Multimedia
* **A/L Entry Requirements:**
  1. Three (3) 'S' passes in **Physical Science** or **Engineering Technology** stream in one sitting; **OR**
  2. Three (3) 'S' passes in **any stream** with an 'S' pass in A/L Information & Communication Technology (ICT); **OR**
  3. Three (3) 'S' passes in **any stream** + O/L Mathematics Credit ('C') + Minimum 50% marks in an university-approved **Bridging Course**.
