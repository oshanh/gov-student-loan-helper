## Introduction

Production of a human capital reserve with high standards is essential for development of a country. Higher education goes beyond basic schooling offering specialized and advanced learning that fosters intellectual development, critical thinking and personal growth. Investment in higher education results in multi-faceted favorable outcomes including enhanced earning potential and quality of life, economic growth and reduced inequality.

The state university system of Sri Lanka is able to serve only 25% of the university qualified students, based on the G.C.E (A/L) results. Majority of the students who are unable to enter into State Universities cannot afford foreign universities, foreign university affiliated local institutes or local non-state degree awarding institutes recognized under Section 25A of the Universities Act No. 16 of 1978. In order to fill this lacuna, Interest Free Student Loan Scheme (IFSLS) is being implemented by the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education as per the direction of budget proposal in the year 2017 with the objective of increasing higher education opportunities for the students partnering with eligible Degree Awarding Institutes (DAIs) under the provisions of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978 (as amended).

Accordingly, the government provides an interest free loan to students subject to a maximum of **Rs. 1,500,000/=** to pursue degree programs with higher market demand and professional value, which are approved by the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education. Further, an interest free loan subject to a maximum of **Rs. 300,000/=** can be obtained by the students at their discretion to cover the additional expenses. The principal loan amount should be paid by the students within seven (7) or eight (8) years after completing the degree programme. The students are given a grace period of one (1) year after completion of the degree programme prior to commencing repayment. The interest will be borne by the government on behalf of the students during the entire loan period which is twelve (12) years.

> **Important:** It is compulsory to register and submit applications through the IFSLS online system. The prospective students of IFSLS are advised to read this handbook thoroughly before filling out their online applications.

## SECTION 1  
### Policies, Rules & Regulations to Follow Undergraduate Degree Programmes at DAIs under IFSLS

### 1.1 Admission Policy for DAIs
The opportunity is given to follow selected degree Programmes at DAIs that are entitled to IFSLS. Selection of students for degree programmes under IFSLS is based on minimum entry qualifications, the preferences of the students, number of applications received and the seating capacity granted by the relevant DAI subject to the number of loans granted by State banks approved by the Cabinet of Ministers for IFSLS. When the number of applications received exceeds the seating capacity of each course at the relevant DAI, the selection will be done on the merit order of Z-score obtained at the G.C.E (A/L) examination by the applicants.

### 1.2 Minimum Qualifications for Admission - Academic year 2026/27
The candidates who have passed G.C.E (A/L) Examination in **2023, 2024 and 2025** conducted by the Department of Examinations in Sri Lanka and have been considered as qualified for university entrance are entitled to apply for IFSLS for the academic year 2026/27, subject to following conditions:

1. Having obtained simple (S) passes for all three subjects in one and the same sitting and not exceeding three sittings.
2. Having a minimum of 30 marks for the Common General Test at any sitting without exceeding three sittings (The result must be obtained at the time of interviews conducted).
3. Minimum of a simple (S) pass for General English at the G.C.E (A/L) Examination **OR** Minimum of a simple (S) pass for English Language at the G.C.E (O/L) Examination.
4. The age of the candidates should be **25 years or below by 27th September, 2026**.

---

### 1.3 Bridging Course

#### 1.3.1 Bridging Course for ICT Degrees
Candidates who have neither qualified for university admission from G.C.E. (A/L) Physical Science stream or Engineering Technology Stream nor obtained at least a simple pass (S) for Information & Communication Technology (ICT) at the G.C.E (A/L) in any attempt at the G.C.E A/L Examination, must follow a bridging course to get the competence of the relevant stream.

**Bridging Course to Computer Science / ICT Degrees:**
* **Foundational Mathematics:** 60 direct contact hours – 4 credits
* **Computational Thinking:** 60 (30T+30P) direct contact hours – 3 credits
* **English and Communication Skills:** 30 direct contact hours – 2 credits

#### 1.3.2 Bridging Course for Management Degrees
Candidates who have neither qualified for university admission from G.C.E. (A/L) Commerce stream nor obtained at least a simple pass (S) in any stream, with “Economics” as a main subject in one and the same sitting, must follow a bridging course to get the competence of the relevant stream.

> **Note:** Kindly reach out to the listed Degree Awarding institutes to obtain detailed information regarding the bridging course offering. By following a Bridging course in any of the mentioned DAIs, a candidate becomes eligible to register for a degree programme in ICT stream/Management stream in any DAI, provided they have fulfilled the other requirements.

### 1.6 Ineligibility Criteria
Reasons for **not** qualifying for admission under the IFSLS to a DAI are:

1. Sitting the G.C.E. (Advanced Level) Examination on **more than three (03) occasions**.
2. Applicants who have already been selected or registered as an internal student to follow a Bachelor’s degree at a state university coming under the UGC based on G.C.E. (A/L) examination results considered for this program (2023, 2024, 2025, or previous years).
3. Applicants selected as internal students to follow a first degree in a state higher educational institute established under any other Acts of Parliament of Sri Lanka.
4. Applicants selected and registered as internal students in a College of Education under the Ministry of Education to follow a diploma course.
5. Applicants selected/registered for a duration of three years or more in full-time Higher National Diploma (HND) courses conducted by SLIATE or NDES at the Institute of Engineering Technology (IET) of NAITA.
6. Applicants registered as internal students to follow a three-year diploma course conducted by a university college established under the University of Vocational Technology.
7. Applicants selected/registered for any other degree program fully or partially funded by public funds.
8. Applicants selected/registered for a Bachelor’s degree under any foreign scholarship program implemented by the Ministry of Education or any other government organization.
9. Applicants already registered for a full-time degree course at a DAI associated with IFSLS.
10. Applicants who have already obtained a Bachelor’s degree or relevant qualification from a higher education institute mentioned in points 2 to 7 above.
11. Applicants who have provided false information and forged documents for admission and registration.

> **Please Note:** If the details provided by the applicant at the time of application, registration, or admission are found incorrect or false, actions will be taken to dismiss the applicant from the IFSLS and the relevant DAI.

### 1.8 Other Important Details for Students

* **Agreements:** Selected students must enter into an agreement with the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, as well as a loan agreement with the lending bank.
* **Guarantors:** The mother/father or legal custodian acts as the **first guarantor**, and a close relative acts as the **second guarantor**.
* **Total Loan Period:** **12 years**.
  * **4-Year Degrees:** 5 years grace period (4 years study + 1 year post-study) followed by **7 years** of repayment (84 equal monthly installments).
  * **3-Year Degrees:** 4 years grace period (3 years study + 1 year post-study) followed by **8 years** of repayment (96 equal monthly installments).
* **Payment Disbursements:** Semester fees are directly disbursed by the bank to the respective DAI at the beginning of each semester upon recommendation by the Ministry.
* **Stipend Loan Option:** Students can opt for an additional interest-free stipend loan of **Rs. 75,000/- per year** (Rs. 300,000 total for 4-year programs; Rs. 225,000 total for 3-year programs). Paid quarterly at **Rs. 18,750/-** (Rs. 6,250/- per month). Repayment follows the same terms as the course fee loan.
* **Academic Standards:** Students must obtain a minimum of a **Credit Pass (C)** in every compulsory subject and maintain **80% attendance**.
* **Repeat Exams:** If a student fails a compulsory subject, they must re-sit in the next semester/academic year to secure at least a "C" pass. The student bears all costs for repeat examinations.
* **Discontinuation & Defaults:**
  * If a student cannot continue, payments are suspended/terminated upon official review.
  * If a student drops out or defaults on repayment after completion, they must repay the outstanding capital plus interest borne by the government up to that date.
  * Failure to settle will result in the student's and guarantors' names being listed in the **Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka (CRIB)**.
* **Overseas Travel:** To depart the country, the student must settle the loan **in full**.

Supervision of the IFSLS and progress reviews are conducted jointly by the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education and the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

### 2.2 Selection of Students for Degree Programmes at DAIs

#### 2.2.1 Eligibility Requirements
Candidates passing G.C.E (A/L) Examinations in **2023, 2024, and 2025** qualified for university entrance can apply if they fulfill general admission requirements (Section 1.2) and program-specific minimum entry qualifications.

#### 2.2.2 Selection Process
1. Applicants may apply for any number of eligible degree programmes; however, an applicant will only be selected for **one** degree programme.
2. Selections are based on applicant preferences and available seating capacity.
3. If applications exceed seating capacity, selection is made based on **Z-Score merit order**.
4. Priority is given to the first preference; if unqualified, subsequent preferences are evaluated in order.
