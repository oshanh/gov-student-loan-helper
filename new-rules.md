# Interest-Free Student Loan Scheme (IFSLS) - Intake 11 (Academic Year 2026/2027)

---

## 1. General Minimum Admission Requirements
To be eligible to apply for any degree program under the Interest-Free Student Loan Scheme (Intake 11), candidates must satisfy all of the following conditions:

1. **A/L Examination Qualification:** Passed the G.C.E. (A/L) Examination in **2023, 2024, or 2025** conducted by the Department of Examinations, Sri Lanka, and been considered qualified for university entrance[cite: 6].
2. **Subject Passes:** Obtained simple (**S**) passes for all three subjects in one and the same sitting (not exceeding three sittings)[cite: 6].
3. **Common General Test:** A minimum of **30 marks** for the Common General Test at any sitting (not exceeding three sittings)[cite: 6].
4. **English Language:** 
   - A minimum of a simple (**S**) pass for General English at the G.C.E. (A/L) Examination; **OR**
   - A minimum of a simple (**S**) pass for English Language at the G.C.E. (O/L) Examination[cite: 6].
5. **Age Limit:** Must be **25 years old or below** by September 27, 2026[cite: 6].

---

## 2. Bridging Course Requirements
Candidates who have not qualified directly through specific subject streams (e.g., non-IT/Physical Science students applying for ICT degrees, or non-Commerce students applying for Management degrees) must follow a bridging course to attain the necessary competence[cite: 7].

### 2.1 Bridging Course for ICT / Computer Science Degrees
* **Target Candidates:** Candidates who have neither qualified for university admission from the G.C.E. (A/L) Physical Science stream or Engineering Technology stream nor obtained at least a simple pass (S) for Information & Communication Technology (ICT) at the G.C.E. (A/L) in any attempt[cite: 7].
* **Course Structure:**
  1. Foundational Mathematics (60 direct contact hours - 4 credits)[cite: 7]
  2. Computational Thinking (60 direct contact hours - 3 credits)[cite: 7]
  3. English and Communication Skills (30 direct contact hours - 2 credits)[cite: 7]
* *Note:* Any candidate who has obtained a simple pass ("S") for ICT in any attempt at the G.C.E. A/L examination is **exempted** from the ICT bridging course[cite: 38].

### 2.2 Bridging Course for Management Degrees
* **Target Candidates:** Candidates who have neither qualified for university admission from the G.C.E. (A/L) Commerce stream nor obtained at least a simple pass (S) in any stream with "Economics" as a main subject in one and the same sitting[cite: 7].
